Making reproductive health information easier to manage.

OUR PURPOSE

Healthcare information shouldn't feel scattered.

Important reproductive-health information can easily become difficult to remember, organize or access. AFRA Connect is designed around a simple idea: make that information easier to manage.

Organize

Keep birth-control information, test-kit records and test results organized in one place.

Protect

Build the platform around privacy, responsible data handling and secure access.

Connect

Create a foundation where users, manufacturers and healthcare providers can eventually connect responsibly.
